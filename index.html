Who we are

Committed to Inspire & Deliver

Established in 2019 in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province, The Sailors ART designs, organizes and operates events that move people — from intimate executive gatherings to multi-zone public festivals welcoming hundreds of thousands of guests.

We are recognized by the Eastern Province Governorate and the General Entertainment Authority, and certified for events, ticketing, crowd management, concerts, expos and incubator facilities. One partner, every deck of the ship.

  • Concept, design, approvals and execution under one roof
  • Proven record in mega events and crowd management
  • Part of The Sailors Group — established 2004
