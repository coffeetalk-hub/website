We are .ART

Your Inspiring Partner

Established in 2019 for talent development and innovation, The Sailors ART is recognized by the Eastern Province Governorate and the General Entertainment Authority, and certified for events, ticketing, crowd management, concerts and expos — with incubator facilities.

We create niche experiences, collaborative platforms and a strong community — building bridges with our extensive resources, providing continuous growth, and telling stories to inspire. Welcome aboard.

  • Mega-event crowd management, ticketing platform, reporting and media coverage
  • Global & domestic partners and alliances in 6+ countries
