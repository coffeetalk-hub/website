Services

Everything your event needs, under one sail

Three service groups that work as one: business and entertainment events, a talent hub and art gallery, and expos and fabrication. Organize, design, obtain approvals, execute — and report.

How we work

From first idea to final report

  1. Listen & planObjectives, audience, budget and KPIs — we chart the course before we set sail.
  2. Design & proposeCreative concept, zoning, programme, and a proposal you can approve with confidence.
  3. Approvals & buildPermits from the relevant authorities, fabrication, ticketing and crowd-management plans.
  4. Execute & reportOperations, media coverage and a post-event report against every agreed target.
