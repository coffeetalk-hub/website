Who we are

Your inspiring partner.

Established in 2019 in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province, recognized by the Eastern Province Governorate and the General Entertainment Authority, and certified for events, ticketing, crowd management, concerts, expos and incubator facilities.

One accountable partner for corporate and public events: we organize, design, obtain approvals and execute — with our own crowd management, ticketing, reporting and media teams. Our record includes 14 multi-zone public mega events, 98 corporate and business events, and a 360-member taskforce on a single event.
