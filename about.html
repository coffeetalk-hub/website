About

Beyond the horizon, since 2019.

The events, entertainment and talent-development arm of The Sailors Group — serving corporate and entertainment clients in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province and beyond.

Our story

Your inspiring partner.

The Sailors ART was established in 2019 for talent development and innovation. We are recognized by the Eastern Province Governorate and the General Entertainment Authority, and certified to deliver events, ticketing, crowd management, concerts and expos — with incubator facilities.

Since then we have hosted, managed and entertained more than 480,000 guests and visitors; managed, designed and operated 14 public and semi-public multi-zone mega events; and designed, operated and managed more than eight business events and venues — across 12 prime cities in Saudi Arabia and Bahrain, with partners in more than six countries.

  • One-stop corporate partner: organize, design, obtain approvals, execute
  • Mega-event crowd management, ticketing platform, reporting and media coverage
  • Events for all ages — cross-industry, cross-country, cross-culture
