Our Story

Sailing since 2019, anchored in a group founded in 2004

The Sailors ART was established in 2019 in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province as the events, entertainment and talent-development arm of The Sailors Group.

Who we are

Your Inspiring Partner

We are a one-stop corporate partner for business and entertainment events — organizing, designing, obtaining approvals and executing everything from executive summits to multi-zone public mega events.

Recognized by the Eastern Province Governorate and the General Entertainment Authority, we are certified for events, ticketing, crowd management, concerts, expos and incubator facilities. Since 2019 we have welcomed more than 480,000 guests across 12 cities in Saudi Arabia and Bahrain, working with partners in six-plus countries.

Our sister companies — The Sailors LLC / ISTA (est. 2004) and The Sailors AI (est. 2021) — give us two decades of corporate experience and a modern technology edge.
