Our Story

Sailing since 2019, anchored in a group founded in 2004

The Sailors ART was established in 2019 in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province as the events, entertainment and talent-development arm of The Sailors Group.

Who we are

Beyond the horizon

The Sailors ART was established in 2019 for talent development and innovation. We are recognized by the Eastern Province Governorate and the General Entertainment Authority, and certified to deliver events, ticketing, crowd management, concerts and expos — with incubator facilities.

Since then we have hosted, managed and entertained more than 480,000 distinguished guests and visitors; managed, designed and operated 14 public and semi-public mega events with multiple zones; and designed, operated and managed more than eight business events and venues — across 12 prime cities in Saudi Arabia and Bahrain.

Our sister companies — The Sailors LLC / ISTA (est. 2004) and The Sailors AI (est. 2021) — give us two decades of industrial and corporate experience and a modern technology edge.

Our manifesto

Life is a journey, not a destination — yet there are many harbors we transit through. We thrive to make the best out of it by achieving Prosperity and reaching Tranquility. Always be Authentic, with the ideal Partnership; Inspire and get inspired.

In this journey we don't control the wind, yet we control the sail. For us to be good sailors and overcome obstacles and challenges, we need to be equipped and well prepared: develop our competencies, have the right skilling, hands-on experience, and think innovatively.

In The Sailors we create niche experiences, collaborative platforms and a strong community — building bridges with our extensive resources, providing continuous growth, and telling stories to inspire.
